*"Traditional."*

That's how Alexei describes their family the night Papa tears it apart. He says it to Ilya early that morning, when they listen through the carpeted floor in the room directly above living room as Mama screams, and pleads, and begs for her dignity, for her sons, and then finally her own life. Papa never deigns a response to her begging, there's only the grunting, and the dense sounds of his fists landing lord-knows where along her body. Ilya is sixteen now, and he knows better. He knows better than to try and help, because when Ilya help it only makes things worse for Mama, and for him, and even Alexei too.

So, while they sit there, the sides of their faces pressed into the rough carpet fibers, Ilya doesn't cry. His lip doesn't even wobble. He just prays. He prays that Mama will stop fighting Papa, just let him have his way, give him what he wants, just be soft and pliant and submissive for him. Placate him with *slick* and *pheromones—*

Ilya gags and Alexei gives him an awkward glance,

*"You don't have to listen to this."* his brother tells him sternly, *"Go finish your studies."*

*"And let Mama be punished, but alone?"*

Beneath them, Mama groans. Or maybe, she moans.

Ilya dry heaves again.

Alexei's eyes slide back to the northern wall, fixed there while he dissociates, *"This is nothing that concerns the likes of a Beta. Do not make yourself suffer."*

Ilya shoves himself away from the floor, *"Because I am a Beta, Papa forcing Mama doesn't concern me? Because you are an Alpha that would do the same to his wife? What would you call that logic?"*

*"Traditional."*

Alexei, though he paints himself in the colors that make Papa smile, is not in truth like him. He grabs Ilya's wrist before his younger brother can lift away from the carpet entirely,

*"We don't have to conform to these traditions."*

And Ilya wretches his forearm away, watching Alexei shuffle upwards; they're the same height even with a five years between them. Ilya's fisted hands spread open when he flings them in dismissive gesture.

*"No, we just watch them. Like late-night reality TV; it doesn't make sense but we keep watching because we can't stop."*

*"No, we stop. We brother, we do."*

*"No,"* Ilya snarls, *"No, you do."*

*"You will not treat your mates in such a way Ilya. This is a rule for both of us."*

Ilya's eyes swarm in their sockets because he can't have mates, he can have a wife or a girlfriend. Betas do not have mates.

*Rules* were put in place to maintain the integrity of machines and processes and people. But rules were also imparted to drag those same three things down into hell and then even further. In Russia, a mated—no bonded—Omega was nothing short of property. In America, from the posts he see on Reddit, Omegas maintained human rights equal to the likings of Alphas. Omegas could roam and walk the streets without being dragged into alleyways, caged in bedrooms, they could shop and smile and drink hot coffee in public on cold days without their mate as a chaperone. He read that re-offending Alphas who had committed heinous assaults on Omegas were required to be muzzled in public. They were certainly not allowed to force Omegas to bond with other Alphas. No tolerance nonconsensual packs.

Ilya's eyes drag across the room to his brother's face, he looks like he wants to cry. He usually does when they can hear Papa having his way with Mama. Ilya wants to hit him right in his stupid mouth, eyes glazed or not, he wonders maybe, if he's upset enough maybe his father or Alexei would turn their nose up to him. Maybe he'd conjure a scent so disruptive and dominate that they'll turn on him and bare their fangs, their eyes will glow gold, their nailbeds would elongate and gnarl into claws. Maybe he could square up with them in that stupid fucking canine-like way that all Alphas carried themselves, constantly challenging proper authority, always pushing themselves onto nonconsenting people. Sure, Alexei isn't like that, not that Ilya had heard or seen anyway, but he's an Alpha all the same.

*"I hate you. Both of you."* Ilya snaps, finally, eyes trickling tears for his mother than Alexei will always be too proud to shed, *"And I will always hate you."*

Alexei only shakes his head, that unruly mops of brown hair bounding around it, *"You don't mean that."*

*"Oh, brother but I do. I hate your little secret-society of asshole-sniffing and self-satisfying, chest thumping neanderthal ways. All you Alphas do is take and take and take——"*

Beneath the carpet Mama wails her worst that night and Ilya's body begins to tremble all the while his voice climbs to drown out Papa. Papa cursing Mama, once again, for bearing him a son born a Beta. He'd rather an Omega daughter, because she can be *used.* She could be bargained with.

*"And you take until there is nothing left but the shell of the person that used to hold their soul and you take even more, somehow."* Ilya can feel his lip trembling but he can't stop, *"You pillage and you hurt and fuck and you inflict trauma in places it should never be and then you don't even have the mental capacity to apologize——"*

The air around him has become heavy, moistened emotion that settles over him like a weighted blanket and makes him feel claustrophobic and damp. He gulps down a breath and exhales another blasphemous string of words that has Alexei on him like the dog he is;

*"Ilyusha, please, he will hear us."*

But Ilya can't stop.

It has been days, weeks, then months, and now finally years of listening to his mother yelp. He's spent sleepless nights watching her tend and lick her own wounds, he's laid down where his father beat him senseless for trying to defend her in his tirades. He searches, parses through something he might've read somewhere, maybe watched someday, on YouTube, something to make sense of why any of this was happening besides Ilya *was born a Beta.* Because you wouldn't beat your most precious person over that would you?

Mama takes Ilya and Alexei to the beaches on days it's especially warm and watches them splash around. She makes sure they *talk,* so that unlike Papa, although she'll never say this, the two of them will be able to communicate outside of secondary gender nuances and pressures. Mama teaches them there's so much more to life than ruining someone else's. There's museums, national parks, observatories, botanical gardens, beaches, military bases, libraries, open markets and bazaars, animal shelters, forests and their accompanying trails, car and airshows, gun shows even. She even takes them to hunt reindeer, and they bring home a bull each and *every* time. Their heads and antlers jut proudly over the family's manor's largest hearth, just for Papa to claim them his own kills right in the face of the three of them.

Then he takes her by the hair, long and curly and golden like Ilya's own, drags her down into the main entryway on the freezing cold marble floor and beats her beneath the crystal chandelier that exposes everything in the room with unforgiving white light until she presents and promises him another son, an Alpha, strong, and big and perfect. He doesn't care that his sons are there to see, or if they see. He doesn't give explanations.

*"Ilya? Where are you going?"*

So why should Ilya?

His feet carry him faster than his mind can, can Papa is just as shocked to see Ilya as his son is to see him, Alexei isn't quick enough to grab his younger brother and they all watch each other in a moment that comes to a head in seconds that pass by like fucking snails. Ilya opens his mouth to say something but he only manages a mangled growl, possibly even a bark. His teeth hurt but his heart hurts more and he moves swiftly to separate his father from his mother.

Papa is bald, so unfortunately there's no hair to grab, but he has a perfectly good neck to snatch—scruff, as an Alpha would say. Ilya's hands are big enough to wrap around it, so he *does.* The man opens his mouth to say something, bristles closely to a mutt that a human could before Ilya drags him down to the floor, and the floor might not be the preferred marble entryway he admires, but it's polished wood, and Ilya makes it work for him.

Their scuffle doesn't amount to much, so less intense than Ilya could ever have imagined that he finds the dismissal of challenge nearly disappointing; has Papa always been this much smaller than him? 

Behind him, Alexei helps Mama, pulls his hoodie off and shoves it over her; she's shaking so badly she can't dress herself. And her pretty white house dress is mottled pink and red from her slick and spit and tears and her eyes aren't that pretty green that phases blue depending on how she feels.

They're grey and unmoving, like the rest of her body.

Much like Papa's now, after Ilya drags him by the throat to the fireplace with the reindeer watching from their mounted placards overhead, and stomps his head into the edge of the neat brick over and over and over and over *and over and over and over and over and over and over———*

Alexei makes good on all that corny ass talking he'd do behind Papa's back when Mama finally wakes up from her injuries; he's inherited Papa's business and siphoned it for all it was worth, and with his familial military benefits and "honor" (none of which he actually had as an actual human being) he plans to send her to some fancy rehabilitation center in *Chicago, Illinois.*

Ilya listens calmly when Alexei explains the new plan of action to them, Mama, eyes barely managing to stay open today, again, and Ilya strewn across half her chest. He's fitted himself up against her and she pets him fondly, running her little thumb across the expanse of his hand while Alexei bickers with him,

*"Will you move off of her, you're heavy."*

Ilya frowns at the brunette; he's taller than Alexei now, by nearly five feet, and just at twenty, *"No, she likes it. She wants me here."*

Mama nods sleepily, feeds them a silly little smile that reaches her eyes, sleepy as she might be, *"Your pheromones are overwhelming Ilya, move now."*

Beneath him, at the mention of pheromones, Mama stills, momentarily, then softens just as quickly. Ilya feels bile wash around his mouth, and his tongue feels thick behind his teeth; maybe Mama is sad her sweet Ilyusha had ultimately ended up a historically late-presenting, *dominate* Alpha. Something worth celebrating for towns over ended up being an international story of devastation and tragedy, and he's glad she couldn't see the headlines one the televisions about them.

He's glad she slept peacefully the last two years.

Anyways, he moves, Ilya retreats to the recliner shoved up against her bed. Right back to where the nurses complain about the men moving it to,

*"Mama,"* Alexei continues with an eye roll so intense it surely leaves him lightheaded, *"Ilya will be going with you. I've got the money to cover his tuition, so he'll be enrolling in a trade school or college as a foreign exchange student."*

Ilya's brows lift at the news. He'd been out of school for two years. Most of that was spent helping Mama, reading to her, watching TV with her, or working out.

*"And you?"* Ilya points lazily to his brother,

Alexei shrugs, *"I'll visit quarterly."*

*"I will need a job then."*

*"Something decent and noninvasive, Ilya. You taking care of mama is your first job."*

Mama shakes her head and both boys roll their eyes this time.

*"I don't like school."*

*"And that's too damn bad isn't it?"*

Ilya groans, his head lolls into the edge of Mama's mattress and her arm sweeps out to pet through his curls.

*"What would I even go to school for?"*

Alexei considers this, then: *"You like cars, motorcycles, ships, planes?"*

Ilya shrugs, *"Yeah. Those are easy."*

*"Mechanical Engineering."* his brother declares.

Mama shoves her thumb up with the hand that had been petting her youngest, she lifts it the highest she had in six months.

Alexei and Ilya exchange gooey glances and warm smiles.
